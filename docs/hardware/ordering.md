# Ordering boards

!!! danger "Do not order boards yet"
    The fabrication files exist and pass their checks, and the layout has had
    an [independent review](../design/hardware-layout-review.md). Nobody has
    yet gone through the checklist that review leaves for a person, and the
    design has never been built. A board ordered after the checklist is a
    first prototype: it may not work, and it may be unsafe to connect.

## Board options

These are what the layout was drawn for. They are JLCPCB's standard options;
none costs extra according to the limits in `hardware/rules/jlcpcb.toml`,
which were read from JLCPCB's capabilities page and its help article on
extra charges on 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11.

| Option | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Layers | 4 | |
| Size | 114 x 110 mm | |
| Thickness | 1.6 mm | The mechanical contract |
| Outer copper | 1 oz (35 um) | The valve paths are calculated for it |
| Inner copper | 0.5 oz, the default | Nothing depends on thicker inner copper |
| Smallest hole | 0.3 mm | JLCPCB charges for a smaller hole and then also selects a costlier material and test. The design rules fail on a hole under 0.3 mm |
| Smallest track and gap | 0.10 mm and 0.2 mm | One piece of track 0.4 mm long is 0.10 mm wide, where the autorouter left a pin; three more of 0.2 mm each are 0.12 to 0.19 mm. Everything else is 0.2 mm or wider. The limit in the rules file is 0.10 mm |
| Smallest text | 1.0 mm high, 0.15 mm line | |

## Assembly

1. Top side only. Every surface-mount part is on the top.
2. The placement files already leave out what the assembler does not place:
   the terminal headers J1 to J4 (through-hole), the bulk capacitor C1 and the
   relay K1 (through-hole, bottom side), and the debug pads J8, which carry no
   part. The BOM still lists C1, K1, J1 to J4 and the display DS1: take those
   lines out in JLCPCB's BOM step.
3. Fit by hand, or ask the assembler: C1 and K1 from the bottom side,
   soldered from the top; J1 to J4 from the top.
4. Run `make cpl-check` in the board directory; it must end with "0 do not
   fit". Then compare the assembler's placement preview with
   `zoneflow-a-pin1.csv` for every polarised part before you confirm. Nobody
   has checked the placement files against a real order.
5. The board has no fiducial marks and the module overhangs the top edge:
   ask for process edges with fiducials on the two 110 mm sides.
6. Check any part the assembler proposes to substitute against the
   [BOM](bom.md). The display panel has no confirmed second source.
7. The display panel DS1 plugs into J7 at final assembly, and the CR2032 cell
   goes into its holder.

## Stock

Stock was checked again on 2026-11-11 for the [cost estimate](cost.md#components).
No line was short of 100 eight-zone boards. The thinnest were the triac
(3,384 for 900 needed), the 48 V TVS diode (5,782 for 1,402) and the display
panel (413 for 100). Check stock again before you order.
